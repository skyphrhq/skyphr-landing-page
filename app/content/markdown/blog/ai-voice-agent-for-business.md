## What is an AI voice agent?

An AI voice agent is a conversational AI system that answers and makes phone calls on behalf of a business. It combines three technologies:

- **Speech recognition** turns the caller's words into text in real time.
- **A large language model (LLM)** understands intent, follows your business rules and decides what to say or do next.
- **Natural text-to-speech** replies in a clear, human-sounding voice.

Connected to your calendar, CRM or helpdesk, the agent doesn't just talk. It acts: it books a slot, logs a lead, sends a confirmation or transfers the call to the right person.

You'll also hear it called an **AI receptionist**, **AI phone agent** or **AI call answering service**. The idea is the same: a virtual team member who never misses a call.

## AI voice agent vs IVR: what's the difference?

Traditional IVR	AI voice agent
How callers interact	Press keys, follow fixed menus	Speak naturally, ask anything
Handles unexpected questions	No	Yes, within your rules
Takes action	Routes calls only	Books, qualifies, logs, transfers
Languages	Pre-recorded per language	Replies in the caller's language, can switch mid-call
Caller experience	Often frustrating	Feels like a real conversation

## How does an AI voice agent work? A typical call

1. **The call connects** on your business number, and the agent greets the caller in your brand's tone.
2. **It understands the need**: a booking, a price question, a support issue or a new enquiry.
3. **It answers or acts.** It checks live availability, shares approved information or collects details.
4. **It confirms** the outcome on the call, for example the booked date and time.
5. **It records everything.** The transcript, caller details and a summary are saved to your system automatically.
6. **It hands off when needed.** Complex or sensitive calls go to your team with full context.

Even a few missed enquiries a week can add up to a significant annual loss, and that's before counting repeat business and referrals. An AI voice agent turns that number into booked work.

## How to choose the right AI voice agent

Before you invest, ask these questions:

- **Does it integrate with your tools?** It should connect to your calendar, CRM, booking platform and helpdesk.
- **Can it follow your rules?** Check that it respects pricing limits, what it may and may not say, and when to escalate.
- **How natural does it sound?** Ask for a live demo, not a recording.
- **Does it handle your callers' languages and accents?** Test it with the kind of callers you actually get.
- **Who owns the data?** Transcripts and lead data should belong to you and be stored securely.
- **Is it custom or a template?** Off-the-shelf bots are quick to start but often can't match your workflows.

A voice agent is usually one part of a bigger system. Your booking flow or CRM may need work too.
