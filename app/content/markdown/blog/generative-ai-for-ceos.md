## What is generative AI?

Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that creates new content, such as text, images, code, or speech, from simple instructions.

In a business setting, it usually has three layers:

- **The model.** A large language model (LLM) such as GPT, Claude, or Gemini understands and generates language.
- **Your data.** Documents, product details, policies, and customer history make the answers specific to your company.
- **The workflow.** Connections to your CRM, helpdesk or ERP let it act, not just answer.

Once connected, generative AI can draft a proposal, answer a customer enquiry, summarise a 40-page contract, qualify a lead or write the first version of code. The real value comes from the workflow, not the chatbot.

You'll also hear it called GenAI, LLM-powered automation or AI copilots. When it takes actions on its own, it's usually called an AI agent.

## Generative AI vs traditional automation: what's the difference?

Aspect	Traditional automation	Generative AI
Input	Structured data, fixed forms	Natural language, documents, images
Rules	Hard-coded if/then logic	Follows instructions and context
New situations	Breaks on exceptions	Adapts within your guardrails
Output	Same result every time	Drafts, answers, summaries, decisions
Best for	Repetitive, predictable tasks	Language-heavy, judgement-based work

## How does generative AI work in a business? A typical workflow

1. An employee or customer starts a task, for example "draft a reply to this complaint".
2. The system pulls context from your approved data, such as past tickets and your refund policy.
3. The model generates a response that follows your instructions, tone and rules.
4. Guardrails check the output for accuracy, sensitive data and policy limits.
5. A team member reviews and approves it, or the system acts directly on low-risk tasks.
6. Every interaction is logged, so you can measure quality and improve over time.

## 10 things CEOs should know about generative AI

1. **Generative AI is a business decision, not an IT project.** The biggest gains come when leadership chooses the problems worth solving, not when a tool is bought and handed to the tech team.
2. **Start with a problem, not a tool.** Look for work that is high-volume, language-heavy and costly, like support tickets, proposals, reports and onboarding. That's where generative AI pays back first.
3. **Your data is your competitive advantage.** Anyone can use a public model. An AI system connected to your own documents and processes is something competitors can't easily copy.
4. **It can be confidently wrong.** Models sometimes produce answers that sound right but are false, known as hallucinations. Grounding answers in your data and keeping people in the loop for high-stakes outputs manages this risk.
5. **AI data privacy and security come first.** Decide what data can go into which tools. Free consumer chatbots are not the place for client contracts or financial records.
6. **Costs are usage-based.** Most models charge per use, so costs grow with volume. Budget for model fees, integration, monitoring and ongoing improvement, not just the build.
7. **Custom beats off-the-shelf for core workflows.** Generic AI tools are fine for personal productivity. For customer-facing or core operations, custom AI solutions fit your rules and systems.
8. **It supports your team before it replaces anyone.** The fastest wins come from removing repetitive work, so your people spend more time on clients and decisions.
9. **Regulation is catching up.** Rules on AI, data protection and copyright keep changing in India, the EU, the US and beyond. Build with clear data ownership and audit trails from day one.
10. **Small pilots win**. A focused pilot on one workflow, with one clear success metric, teaches you more than a year of strategy decks.

## The hidden cost of waiting

Try this simple calculation for your business:

> **Hours spent on repetitive tasks per week × number of people doing them × hourly cost × 52 = annual cost of manual work**

Generative AI won't remove all of those hours. But automating even part of them frees real budget and capacity. Meanwhile, competitors who start now are building the data, skills and processes that take time to catch up with. The cost of waiting isn't just money. It's lost learning time.

## How to choose the right generative AI project

Before you invest, ask these questions:

- **What problem are we solving, and how will we measure it?** Time saved, faster responses, or more leads converted. Pick one metric.
- **Is our data ready?** AI is only as good as the information it can access. Scattered or outdated data needs cleanup first.
- **Where does our data go?** Check where it's stored, who can access it, and whether it's used to train third-party models.
- **Does it integrate with our existing systems?** A tool that sits outside your CRM, ERP, or helpdesk creates more work, not less.
- **Who reviews the output?** Define which tasks need human approval and which can run on their own.
- **Can it scale?** A pilot should be built so it can grow into a full system without starting over.

## Frequently asked questions

How much does generative AI cost for a business?
It depends on the scope. A focused pilot on one workflow costs far less than a full platform. Ongoing costs include model usage, hosting and improvements, so plan a monthly budget, not just a one-time build.

How long does it take to implement generative AI?
A pilot on a single workflow can often go live in a few weeks. Larger systems with several integrations take longer, depending on how ready your data is.

Will generative AI replace my employees?
In most businesses, it takes over repetitive tasks rather than full roles. Your team spends less time drafting, searching, and copying data, and more time on clients and decisions.

Is our company data safe with generative AI?
It can be, with the right setup. Use business-grade models with clear data protection terms, control access by role, and keep sensitive data out of public tools.

Can generative AI work with our existing software?
Yes. Through APIs and integrations, it connects to CRMs, ERPs, helpdesks, websites, and internal tools. This is usually where most of the business value comes from.

Does generative AI work in Hindi, Gujarati, and other languages?
Modern models handle many languages, including Indian regional languages, though quality varies by language and task. Test with real examples from your customers before a full rollout.
