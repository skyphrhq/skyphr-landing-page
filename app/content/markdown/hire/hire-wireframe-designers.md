## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why should I hire a wireframe designer before UI design?

Wireframe designers help define structure, functionality, and user journeys before visual design begins, reducing costly revisions later in the project.

### What is the difference between wireframes and UI design?

Wireframes focus on layout, workflows, and functionality, while UI design focuses on visual appearance, branding, colors, and aesthetics.

### Can you create wireframes for SaaS products?

Yes. We specialize in SaaS wireframing, including dashboards, user roles, workflows, onboarding systems, and complex product structures.

### Do you provide mobile app wireframes?

Yes. We design wireframes for iOS, Android, and cross-platform mobile applications.

### Can wireframes be converted into prototypes?

Absolutely. We can transform wireframes into interactive prototypes for testing, validation, and stakeholder presentations.

### What tools do your wireframe designers use?

We primarily use Figma, Adobe XD, Sketch, Balsamiq, Axure RP, Miro, and other leading UX design tools.

### Can I hire a dedicated wireframe designer?

Yes. Skyphr offers dedicated wireframe designers, project-based engagements, and team augmentation models.

### How long does a wireframing project take?

Project timelines depend on complexity, but most wireframing engagements range from a few days for MVP concepts to several weeks for enterprise platforms.
