## What happens in 90 seconds

One real conversation can go from the first ring to a qualified lead, booked appointment, and complete call record.

### 0:00 — The Phone Rings

Sky answers incoming calls immediately, whether the call comes during business hours, after hours, weekends, or when your team is already busy.

### 0:03 — Greets Your Customer

Sky answers using your business name, approved greeting, tone, services, and communication style.

### 0:12 — Understands What They Need

Sky listens to the caller and understands the reason for the call. It can ask relevant follow-up questions, collect important details, and identify whether the caller wants an appointment, quote, consultation, information, or human follow-up.

### 1:10 — Books the Meeting

When an appointment or meeting is required, Sky checks your connected calendar, provides available options, confirms the selected time with the caller, and books the appointment.

### 1:30 — Saves the Lead

After the call, the important information is available for your team, including the caller details, conversation transcript, enquiry information, and appointment details.

## A Voice Agent You Stay in Control Of

Sky talks to your customers on behalf of your business, so its conversations need to follow your rules. Your business controls what Sky knows, what it can say, what it can do, and when it should hand a conversation to your team.

### Answers From Your Approved Business Information

Sky works from the business information, services, hours, policies, FAQs, and responses you provide. This helps keep conversations aligned with your business instead of allowing the AI voice agent to invent unsupported information.

In practice: “If a customer asks about a service your business does not offer, Sky can clearly explain that the service is unavailable rather than making up an answer.”

### Says When It Doesn't Know

When a question falls outside Sky's approved information or capabilities, it can acknowledge the limitation and capture a message for your team.

In practice: “I'll pass that to the team and they'll get back to you today.”

### Confirms Before It Books

Sky confirms the appointment details with the caller before creating the booking. This confirmation step helps prevent accidental bookings and unnecessary calendar changes.

In practice: “Thursday at 4:30 PM for 30 minutes. Shall I book that for you?”

### Stays Within the Scope You Set

You decide what Sky can discuss and what actions it can perform. Sky can be configured to avoid providing professional advice such as legal, medical, or financial advice and instead escalate those requests to the appropriate person.

In practice: “When a customer asks a question outside the approved scope, Sky can provide the approved response or request a human follow-up.”

### Every Call Is On Record

Call transcripts and lead information provide visibility into customer conversations and help your team review how the AI voice agent is performing.

In practice: “If you identify a better answer or update your business information, Sky's configuration can be updated so future conversations follow the revised information.”

### Your Call Data Stays Yours

Your customer conversations and lead information are valuable business data. Skyphr is designed so your call records and lead information can be used by your team for follow-up, operations, and customer management.

In practice: Your leads, your conversations, and your follow-up process remain part of your business workflow.

### What is an AI voice agent?

An AI voice agent is an artificial intelligence system that can answer and manage phone conversations in real time. Sky can understand customer requests, answer approved questions, capture information, qualify enquiries, book appointments, and escalate calls when human assistance is required.

### What can Sky's AI voice agent do?

Sky can answer business calls, handle common customer questions, qualify leads, capture caller information, book and reschedule appointments, schedule consultations, collect enquiry details, and save call transcripts for your team.

### Can Sky answer calls 24/7?

Yes. Sky is designed to handle business calls around the clock, including after-hours and periods when your team is unavailable. This allows businesses to maintain a consistent phone response without requiring staff to answer every call manually.

### Can an AI voice agent book appointments?

Yes. Sky can connect with scheduling systems such as Cal.com to check availability, offer appointment times, confirm the caller's choice, and create the booking.

### Can Sky connect with our CRM or business tools?

Sky can be configured around your existing business workflow. Depending on your technology stack and requirements, Skyphr can evaluate integrations with calendars, CRMs, lead-management systems, and other business tools.

No. Sky can be configured to stay within an approved business scope and escalate questions that require professional judgment to an appropriate human team member.

Yes. Sky can ask predefined qualification questions, collect contact information, understand the reason for the enquiry, capture relevant requirements, and provide your team with structured information for follow-up.

### Can Sky be customized for my business?

Yes. Sky can be configured around your business name, greeting, services, FAQs, opening hours, policies, qualification questions, booking process, escalation rules, and approved responses.

### Which businesses can use an AI voice agent?

AI voice agents can support many businesses that depend on inbound phone calls, including healthcare clinics, dental practices, law firms, home service companies, real estate agencies, salons, spas, agencies, and consultancies, as well as other service-based businesses.

### Can Sky handle calls when our team is already busy?

Yes. Sky can answer calls when your staff are serving customers, attending meetings, working on-site, or otherwise unavailable. This helps businesses reduce missed calls and capture more enquiries without interrupting ongoing work.
