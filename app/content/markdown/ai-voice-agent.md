## What happens in 90 seconds

One real call, from the first ring to a meeting on your calendar and a lead in your records.

1. **0:00 The phone rings.** Sky picks up on the first ring, whether it's 3 PM or 3 AM. No hold music, no voicemail.
2. **0:03 Greets as your business.** Your business name, your tone, your opening line. Callers hear your front desk, not a robot.
3. **0:12 Understands what they need.** Sky listens, asks the follow-up questions your team would ask, and picks out the details that matter.
4. **1:10 Books the meeting.** It checks your real availability, offers a slot, and books it only after the caller says yes.
5. **1:30 Saves the record.** The transcript, the lead details and the booking land in your records the moment the call ends.

## A voice agent you stay in control of

Sky talks to your customers, so it plays by your rules. Here's how we keep every call accurate, honest and on-brand.

- **Answers only from your business profile.** Sky works from the services, hours, policies and answers you approve. It doesn't make things up to keep the conversation going. In practice: asked about a service you don't offer, Sky says so plainly.
- **Says when it doesn't know.** If a question falls outside what you've given it, Sky doesn't guess. It takes a message so the right person can call back. In practice: "I'll pass that to the team and they'll get back to you today."
- **Confirms before it books.** Sky reads the date and time back to the caller and books only after they say yes. Nothing lands on your calendar by assumption. In practice: "Thursday at 4:30 PM, 30 minutes. Shall I book it?"
- **Stays within the scope you set.** You decide what Sky can talk about. It won't give legal, medical or financial advice, or promise prices you haven't approved. In practice: pricing questions get your approved answer, or a callback.
- **Every call is on record.** Each call is saved with its transcript, so you can see exactly what Sky said and adjust its profile whenever you want. In practice: spot a better answer? Update the profile and Sky uses it next call.
- **Your call data stays yours.** Transcripts and lead details are saved to your records for your team to use. We don't sell or share your callers' information. In practice: your leads, your follow-ups, your data.
